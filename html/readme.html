6. The 7-Color Framework

  teams of Cybersecurity

- Red Team: Attacking systems to find weaknesses.


- Blue Team: Defending systems against active threats.


- Purple Team: Maximizing both attack and defense capabilities together.


- Yellow Team: Building and developing secure software code.


- Green Team: Integrating defense tactics into the building process.


- Orange Team: Teaching builders how to think like attackers.


- White Team: Managing compliance, logistics, and overall strategy.
