The Classic Hacker Ethic

The old hacker ethic states: 

Open access, free flow of information, meritocracy, decentralization.


I support this part: 

Distrust authoritys – promote decentralization.


Judge a hacker by what they do, and not by common criteria such as appearance, age, origin, species, gender, or social status.


here are newer rules of ehthical hacking:

> 1. Never test without explicit consent.

> 2. Respect the boundaries. (Scope)

> 3. Report, don't exploit.

> 4. Protect data and system stability.
